The Hague calls the Rohingya’s suffering genocide, three leaders meet in Shenzhen, and the Gulf crisis widens to Kuwait and Bahrain.
LEAD · THE HAGUE

Genocide, the world court rules

The International Court of Justice rules that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017, in the case brought by Gambia in 2019. The verdict comes nine years after the army’s campaign drove hundreds of thousands from Rakhine state.

LEAD · SHENZHEN

Three leaders, one declaration

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin hold the first-ever three-way summit in Shenzhen and issue a four-point “Declaration toward Peace”, pledging further talks on reducing nuclear risks. It does not mention Ukraine or Iran. Trump met Putin for about 40 minutes after talks with Xi.

LEAD · HORMUZ

The Gulf crisis widens

Iran widens its attacks across the Gulf with fresh strikes on Kuwait and Bahrain. Tanker attacks mount in the Strait of Hormuz: an ADNOC Logistics vessel is hit, 11 crew injured and one missing. The IEA calls it the greatest threat ever observed to energy security; the U.S. blockade of Iran continues.
